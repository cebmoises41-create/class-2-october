Verbos regulares e irregulares

📖 EXPLICACIÓN: PAST PARTICIPLES

🔹 ¿Qué es el Past Participle?

El past participle es la tercera forma del verbo (columna 3). Se usa en:

• Present Perfect: I have eaten sushi.

• Passive Voice: The book was written by her.

1️⃣ Verbos REGULARES

Se forma agregando -ed al infinitivo. El past simple y el past participle son iguales.

Infinitive + ed → Past Simple = Past Participle
✅ play → played → played
✅ accept → accepted → accepted
✅ carry → carried → carried
✅ stop → stopped → stopped

2️⃣ Verbos IRREGULARES

No siguen una regla fija. El past simple y el past participle pueden ser diferentes (o a veces iguales).

Infinitive → Past Simple → Past Participle (forma única)
✅ go → went → gone
✅ write → wrote → written
✅ eat → ate → eaten
✅ buy → bought → bought (iguales)
